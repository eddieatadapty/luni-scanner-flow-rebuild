# Scanner Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/luni-scanner-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Scanner users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Scanner's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Ask what they scan, then say it back | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | A first scan before the first ask | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Show what Pro adds to the scan they just made | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Make the yearly saving impossible to miss | +10–15% ARPU, more yearly plans |
| 05 | Catch the people who close the paywall | +10–15% ARPU from people who said no |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Scanner's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Scanner. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
